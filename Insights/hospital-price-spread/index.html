The spread
Employers pay these hospitals about 1.75× Medicare. Medicare Advantage pays Medicare, give or take 5%.
Every MA carrier's median hospital rate sits between 98% and 105% of Medicare. The real spread is employer against everyone else, so an MA plan cannot buy its way into a hospital on rate alone.

What it shows
  • MA is priced at Medicare: median MA rate is 100% of the hospital's own Medicare rate on every service above. Employers pay 1.7× on DRGs, 2.2× to 2.5× outpatient.

  • Columbus employers pay 80% to 96.5% of billed charges: a median 2.5× Medicare, vs 1.7× across NE. CT chest: $2,166 employer, $119 Medica Cost, $116 Medicare.

Disclosure
Geography
IA + NE hospitals on CMS's list: 211. Files parsed 195 (109 IA, 86 NE); 5 failed, 11 not found.
Original Medicare
2026 Medicare rate for that hospital's CCN: FY2026 IPPS per MS-DRG, CY2026 OPPS per CPT/HCPCS. Not the file's own Medicare line.
Hospitals
Only PPS hospitals have a fee-schedule rate: 55 in IA/NE, 52 parsed, 39 priced by both MA and employer on 100+ codes. The 144 critical access hospitals are paid at cost: in no % figure.
Payer class
The book's keyword map on payer + plan name (MAPD, GRP). Carrier by payer name, as in the RPI MA-vs-Medicare study.
Method
Facility dollar rates, no modifiers, 20–500% of Medicare. Median plan rate ÷ Medicare per hospital × code × class; hospital = median over codes both classes price.
Listed
Hospitals whose file names the carrier as an MA payer: a floor. 160 of 195 files name one.
Known gaps
Published rates, not claims. Medica Cost Plan is a Section 1876 cost plan, listed under MA in the file.
Sources
Hospital price files (45 CFR 180) via /cms-hpt.txt: atlas_external.hpt_book, hpt_sources, ma_pa_hospital_payers. CMS 2026 IPPS/OPPS by CCN: medfee_ipps, medfee_opps. Pull: DEVOTED-JORDAN/hpb/, build_hospital_price_book.py
