Read with care

Data-quality flags stay in

462 plan × hospital pairs carry a ⚠ flag, where one setting's median is under 60% or over 200% of Medicare (for example Wellpoint inpatient case rates in the CHI Health files, about 27%). They stay in the numbers so the problem is visible.

Disclosure
Geography
Iowa and Nebraska: hospitals (CCN), physician fees (PFS locality), DME (state).
Data years
PFS, OPPS, ASC: CY2026–27. IPPS: FY2026–27 (federal fiscal years). CLFS, DMEPOS: CY2026. Actual paid: CMS utilization 2024. Prior auth: calendar 2025.
MA rate
MA negotiated dollars in the hospitals' own price files (payer type MAPD) ÷ Original Medicare, same hospital and code. Professional fees vs the PFS facility rate.
Codes
The 50 codes most often priced for MA per setting (inpatient DRG, outpatient, professional, lab).
Denominator
Medicare's actual average paid for that hospital and code if any; else the 2026 fee schedule.
Thresholds
Code ratios outside 20%–500% dropped. ⚠ where a setting's median is under 60% or over 200%: 462 pairs, kept in.
80.6%
Median, across plan × hospital pairs, of each pair's median code ratio (IQR 78.2%–92.2%).
Sample sizes
2,699 pairs (Iowa 2,445, Nebraska 254): 369 MA plans, 114 hospitals. Hospital-specific inpatient and outpatient rates: 59 hospitals.
Prior auth
Each insurer's own share of standard requests denied (CMS-0057-F), weighted by its contracts' IA or NE enrollment (Nov 2026); insurer-wide, not per hospital. Not posted: Wellmark Advantage, Medica, Medical Associates.
Known gaps
No public hospital-level denial rate. Outpatient actuals exist only for comprehensive APCs. Critical access hospitals have no fee-schedule baseline.
Sources
CMS PFS, IPPS, OPPS/ASC, CLFS and DMEPOS fee schedules; CMS Provider Utilization & Payment (2024); hospital price files (atlas_external.hpt_book; view ma_pct_of_medicare); insurer CMS-0057-F postings (CY2025); CMS MA enrollment (Nov 2026); KFF (Aug 2026).
