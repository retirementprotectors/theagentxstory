Disclosure
Geography
Iowa and Nebraska acute hospitals with MAPD and group dollar rates for the same code in their own file: 26 IA, 18 NE.
Match
Same hospital (CCN), same code, same setting: MS-DRG inpatient, CPT/HCPCS facility outpatient. No professional fees.
Medicare rate
That hospital's 2026 fee schedule (IPPS by CCN + DRG incl. wage index, IME, DSH, capital; OPPS by CCN + HCPCS; lab = CLFS), not the file's own Medicare column. Cost-paid Critical Access Hospitals drop out.
MAPD / Group
MAPD = payers typed Medicare Advantage + 'MCR ADV' rows. Group = employer/commercial group, TPAs + 'COMM' rows. ACA, individual, Medicaid out.
Clean rules
Dollar rates only (no % of charges, per diem); each rate 20–500% of Medicare; MA plan × hospital × setting medians outside 60–200% dropped, group under 60% dropped. Cell = median across plans. Page 2: no lab cells; hospitals with 100+ codes; carrier Group = group % on that carrier's cells.
CCH estimate
Columbus posts commercial only as % of billed charges: BCBS $ = 89% × its median gross charge for the code.
Carrier side (TiC)
Wellmark TiC (Iowa only): 173,075 facility cells at 115 hospitals, mostly drugs and vaccines, none of the page-1 codes; inpatient is APR-DRG. Group side = hospital file in both states.
Sources
Hospital machine-readable price files (45 CFR 180), atlas_external.hpt_book / hpt_rates with hpt_payer_types; medfee_ipps (FY2026), medfee_opps, medfee_clfs (CY2026); Wellmark TiC: price_join_summary.
