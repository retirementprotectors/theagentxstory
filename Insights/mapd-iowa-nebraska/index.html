Disclosure
Geography
Iowa and Nebraska only. Page 1: Platte, NE, its 8 bordering counties, 4 NE metros and Iowa's 5 largest MA counties (by counted members). Page 2: contracts with IA or NE members.
Data years
County rates: CMS 2027 rate book (2024 for the change). Payment, risk, rebate, Part D subsidy: CMS plan payment 2024. Members, eligibles, penetration: November 2026. Stars: 2026 and 2027. MLR: contract year 2023. Endings: 2026→2027 crosswalk.
Rates
Parts A+B monthly rate per member at a 1.0 risk score, by bonus level (0%, 3.5%, 5%), MIPPA IME removed, ESRD excluded. Equal across the three levels = CMS's benchmark cap.
Plans included
Members: Medicare Advantage, individual plans (EGHP, PDP excluded; Cost plans shown on their own rows). County pay: non-SNP HMO and Local PPO averages.
Weighting
State 2027 rates weighted by each county's MA enrollees (penetration file). Contract pay weighted by each plan's Nov 2026 IA+NE members; * = contract mean of all 2024 plans.
QBP
5% bonus at overall stars of 4 or more; capped counties get none. New contracts and contracts without enough data follow separate CMS rules, shown as new / n/a. Rebate share: 50% under 3.5 stars, 65% at 3.5–4, 70% at 4.5+.
Excluded
CMS hides plan × county cells of 10 or fewer, so member counts are floors.
Known gaps
No 2028 rate book yet (CMS publishes it in April 2027). Payment data is two years behind; MLR three. A/B pay excludes the rebate, which CMS reports separately.
Sources
CMS Medicare Advantage Rate Book 2027 and 2024 (county capitation rates); CMS Plan Payment Data, payment year 2024 (plan and county); CMS Monthly Enrollment by Contract/Plan/State/County, November 2026; CMS MA State/County Penetration, November 2026; CMS Star Ratings 2026 and 2027; CMS contract MLR, contract year 2023; CMS 2026→2027 plan crosswalk.
