The hurdle, in one number
The gap is one point of Medicare.
Every priced Medica line in Columbus's file reads "Payer contract is 103.00% of Medicare" (median of 164 codes: 102.5%). 11 NE CHI hospitals' files already pay Devoted 104%. One point on Columbus's 17 largest Medicare DRGs ($4.19M allowed, 2024) is about $42K a year.

Geography

Period
Price files as posted (Columbus 2026-03-30). Enrollment, penetration Nov 2026. CMS payment 2024. Plan status: 2027 crosswalk.
Plans
MA individual plans, all types and SNPs; no PDP, Cost or employer plans. Medica's cost plans are sold in 25 NE counties, not Platte.
Suppression
CMS hides plan × county cells of 10 or fewer: every count is a floor.
Hospital rates
% of Medicare: median code-level MA rate ÷ fee schedule, 20–500% kept. × Medica: payer % × gross charge ÷ Medica $, median outpatient line.
Dollars
CMS A/B payment: county average for local non-SNP PPOs × 12 × members. One point: 1% of Medicare allowed on Columbus's 17 DRGs with more than 10 FFS stays (2024).
Known gaps
Price files are not network directories. No Medica or Devoted prior-auth data published. No inpatient commercial dollars (Columbus posts DRG lines without gross charges).
